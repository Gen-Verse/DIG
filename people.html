People

A small group for ambitious research.

DIG is being built around researchers who want to define problems, build systems, and push ideas far enough that others can reuse them. The group welcomes students and researchers across algorithms, systems, and scientific discovery.

Founder & Principal Investigator

Ling Yang

Incoming Assistant Professor (PI), Researcher, and PhD advisor. Currently a Postdoctoral Researcher in ECE / Princeton AI Lab at Princeton University, working with Prof. Mengdi Wang. He received his PhD from Peking University, advised by Prof. Bin Cui and Prof. Luxia Zhang, and was selected for the ByteDance Top Seed Talent Program during his doctoral studies.

His research focuses on LLM / Agent Post-Training, Reinforcement Learning Systems / Infrastructure, Recursive Self-Improvement, and AI for Discovery. He founded DIG at Peking University and established the Gen-Verse open research community.
