# DIG — Discovery Intelligence Group

**Peking University · Beijing, China**

> Building learning, evolving, and discovery infrastructure for next-generation models and agents.

**Discovery Intelligence Group (DIG)** is a research group at Peking University led by **Ling Yang (杨灵)**. We study how foundation models and agents can move beyond static problem solving toward systems that **learn over long horizons, interact reliably, improve themselves continuously, and participate in open-ended discovery**.

## Research

| Direction | Focus |
|---|---|
| **LLM / Agent Post-Training** | Reasoning, agentic behavior, reward / verifier design, process supervision, long-horizon credit assignment, environment interaction and generalization. |
| **RL Systems / Infrastructure** | Unified infrastructure for large-scale RL and agent training, including asynchronous training, long-horizon rollouts, multi-environment learning and distributed inference / training. |
| **Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI)** | Experience-, memory-, skill-, data- and environment-driven self-improvement; environment–policy co-evolution; active exploration; self-evolving harnesses. |
| **AI for Discovery** | Models that discover and formulate problems, construct representations, form hypotheses, design interventions and experiments, and revise research processes against external evidence. |

## Open Research

DIG develops open research systems and community infrastructure together with **[Gen-Verse](https://github.com/Gen-Verse)**. Our goal is to connect:

**Research ideas → Reusable systems → Open-source community**

Selected systems across the Gen-Verse ecosystem include **ReasonFlux, RLAnything, OpenClaw-RL, GenEnv, MMaDA, dLLM-RL** and more.
